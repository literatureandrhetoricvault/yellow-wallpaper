Historical framing note:
The game intentionally does NOT teach that every postpartum mental-health condition was simply called "hysteria." Nineteenth-century physicians did recognize childbirth-related mental disturbances, but used period-specific categories and theories. The goal is to give students enough temporal/cultural setting to analyze the story accurately without assigning the narrator a modern diagnosis.
